# Dorm Drop SLO — Image direction

Generation method: built-in ImageGen tool. Six product raster assets were generated for this website; the original PNG files remain unchanged. The product atlas is displayed through CSS positions, without altering or slicing the source image. All imagery is concept styling, not an exact product specification.

## Shared prompt direction

The following shared text was prepended to each of the four asset briefs.

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: premium ecommerce campaign photography for Dorm Drop SLO, dorm essentials for students arriving in California. Art direction: exceptionally polished contemporary creative-agency still life, tactile textiles, quiet architecture, sculptural product arrangement, directional warm Californian studio daylight from upper left, soft but distinct long shadows. True photorealism, close attention to material, soft cotton waffle and rib weave, matte forest green (#244732), ivory, natural oat, tiny pale citrus accents. No plastic CGI look, no fake branding. No writing, no typography, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no watermarks, no logos, no collage, no frames. Every object completely inside composition, generous margins; products are hero, clear at thumbnail size. Landscape 4:3.

## First Night brief

One highly composed editorial studio product still life of the First Night kit, on a seamless warm pale butter/ivory plaster floor and wall. EXACT main groups: a neatly folded deep forest-green duvet and ivory fitted sheet topped with one plump ivory pillow; a stack of two cream bath towels; three unlabelled neutral travel toiletry bottles; a small ivory US wall charger with neatly coiled white USB-C cable; an olive shower caddy with shower sandals tucked alongside. Arrange as an elegant asymmetric sculpture, bedding left center, smaller objects extending right foreground. Camera 35-degree above eye-level studio tabletop. All items have visual breathing room, premium real objects. Do not add lamps, baskets, fans, rugs or storage boxes.

## Product atlas brief

This is a precisely aligned ecommerce PRODUCT ATLAS, not a single scene. Full image aspect 4:3. EXACTLY FOUR equal columns and THREE equal rows: 12 equal square cells with invisible boundaries. Each cell background is identical pale cream #f4f1e6, perfectly even camera and subtle bottom-left contact shadow. Each independent product fully centered in its own square, with at least 18 percent margin on all four sides, no object crosses a cell. Each photographed as a premium isolated real product at gentle 20-degree top view.

Row 1 left to right: (1) forest-green folded bedding with ivory pillow and sheet, (2) stack of two ivory towels, (3) three blank cream/sage toiletry bottles, (4) white US wall charger and coiled white cable.

Row 2 left to right: (5) sage shower caddy with olive shower sandals, (6) sage adjustable desk lamp, (7) oat canvas laundry hamper, (8) five light wooden clothes hangers.

Row 3 left to right: (9) matte forest-green reusable water bottle, (10) small sage retro desk fan, (11) rolled cream woven rug, (12) low rectangular oat under-bed canvas storage box with lid.

CRITICAL: strictly uniform 4-column by 3-row geometry and order, no labels, no cell lines, no borders. Objects completely contained within their cells. Ultra high resolution crisp product photography.

## Where the images are used

The three package scenes appear in the kit collection, selected-kit summary and confirmation. The First Week scene also serves as the homepage hero. The atlas supplies the product gallery, product-detail dialog, builder sidebar, roommate-item thumbnails, custom-kit previews and saved order contents.

The live 3D room is drawn from the actual room model, rather than from a generated room rendering. Its marketing poster mode does not change the shopper’s saved kit.
